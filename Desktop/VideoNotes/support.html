Frequently Asked Questions

Where are my videos stored?

All your videos and notes are stored locally on your device. They are never uploaded to any external servers, ensuring your privacy.

How do I delete a video note?

Swipe left on any video note in your list and tap the delete button to remove it.

Can I backup my video notes?

Your video notes are included in your device's iCloud backup if you have iCloud Backup enabled in your device settings.

Is my data private?

Yes! VideoNotes does not collect any personal information. All your content stays on your device.
