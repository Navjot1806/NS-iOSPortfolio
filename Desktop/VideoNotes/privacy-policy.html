Privacy Policy

VideoNotes App

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how VideoNotes handles your information.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal data.

VideoNotes is designed with your privacy in mind. All your videos and notes are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded to our servers.

Data Storage

  • All videos are stored locally on your device
  • All notes are stored locally on your device
  • No data is transmitted to external servers
  • No analytics or tracking tools are used

Third-Party Services

VideoNotes does not integrate with any third-party services that collect your personal information.

Data Security

Since all data remains on your device, your content is protected by your device's built-in security features, including passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID.

Children's Privacy

VideoNotes does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 years of age.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
